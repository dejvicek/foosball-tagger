How to tag
S
Ball is set: possession starts. Pressing S again after a tagged shot saves it and starts the next one.
F
Shot released: possession ends.
N
Possession ended without a shot (lost ball, pass, time). Saves immediately.
Enter
Save the tagged shot. Missing fields stay blank and don't count in percentages.
Space
Play or pause.
← →
1 second back or forward; with Shift, 5 seconds.
, .
One frame back or forward (set your frame rate in the controls).
[ ]
Slower or faster playback.
U
Delete the last saved possession.

Pressing a tag key a second time clears that field. Click any time in the log to jump back to that possession.
